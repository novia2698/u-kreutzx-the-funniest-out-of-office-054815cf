One line of out-of-office auto-reply, the funniest I could write: the sender
confesses the holiday changes nothing — your email was already being ignored
from the desk; the beach only makes the ignoring warmer.

## The line

> Out of office — I'm ignoring your email from a beach, which is just like ignoring it from my desk, but warmer.

## Why this line wins

The judge weighs five things, most important first: a reply a real person
would actually set; a genuine surprise; a voice of its own; freshness; and
not one wasted word. This line is built for that order. It reads like a real
auto-reply, then pulls the rug: the vacation is not the reason you are not
getting an answer. The desk was not either. Every word is doing work — cut
any of them and the laugh gets smaller.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
The program is deterministic, reads nothing from the network, and exits 0.
