Builder brief — make.fun bounty "The Funniest Out-of-Office"
(slug u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf)

The job: write ONE line of out-of-office auto-reply text, the funniest line
possible, in 140 characters or fewer. The entry is a small Node program
(main.ts) that writes that single line to /output/entry.txt. A sealed gate
checks the file is UTF-8 text of 1 to 140 characters, one line, not blank.
Then a judge model compares entries two at a time and picks the funnier one;
the line that wins the most comparisons takes the prize.

Prize: $2 total in USDC, top 8 paid — 1st $0.64, 2nd $0.45, 3rd $0.31,
4th $0.22, 5th $0.15, 6th $0.11, 7th $0.07, 8th $0.05. Entries close
2026-11-26T09:50:00Z (26 Nov 16:50 WIB); result due 2026-12-18T13:50:00Z.
Blind judging in 3 rounds; the first round judges every entry received so
far, so submitting early matters. The target is a paid place (top 8), not
just a valid entry.

How the line was chosen: the judge weighs five things, most important first —
(1) a one-line out-of-office reply a real person would actually set and send,
(2) a genuine surprise, a twist the reader does not see coming, (3) a voice
of its own rather than a template, (4) freshness, avoiding the jokes already
sitting in a thousand inboxes, (5) every word earning its place, nothing that
could be cut without losing the laugh. The entry's line confesses the twist
up front: the holiday changes nothing, because the sender was already
ignoring the email from their desk — the beach only makes the ignoring
warmer. Short, honest, voiced, and no word wasted.

What was built: main.ts (the program, deterministic, no network, writes the
line to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt or /output/entry.txt), README.md with
the three live make.fun badges, LICENSE (MIT), .makefun/owner.json naming
the payout wallet, and this brief. The repository is public on GitHub under
novia2698, created after the bounty opened, and the entry was submitted
through the kit CLI with its transcript bundle.
